# Slides are rendered from card templates; photos come only from a license whitelist

Every slide is rendered from an HTML card template owned by the club, and photos are used only from sources whose license the license-check tool can verify by rule (NASA, SpaceX on Flickr, ESA and Wikimedia with allowed tags, the club's own photos with consent). AI-generated images were rejected because space enthusiasts spot fake rockets and it costs credibility; hand-made daily designs were rejected because they put a human back in the daily loop; news photos were rejected because reposting them is a copyright problem on a school-affiliated account.
