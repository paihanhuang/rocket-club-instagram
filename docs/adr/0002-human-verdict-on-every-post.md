# Nothing publishes without a human verdict

Every draft waits in the Discord approval channel for an approver's verdict, and the publisher only ever publishes approved, unexpired drafts. Full automation was the original ask; the user chose the human gate because a wrong launch date or an unlicensed photo on a school-affiliated account costs more than two minutes a day of tapping. Low-risk pillars may be moved to auto-publish later, one at a time, after a clean month.
