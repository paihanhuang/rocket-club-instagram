# Fence: hard rules a draft may never break

The plan can ask for anything. These rules win. A draft that trips one gets a
flag the approver must see, and the approve reaction means every flag was
checked by a human.

1. A human approves every post. Nothing publishes on its own.
2. No student's face or full name without that student's consent. Club photos
   come only from the consented folder.
3. No automated direct messages, comments, follows, or likes. Ever.
4. No politics, no elections, no religion.
5. No jokes about launch failures, accidents, or people being hurt.
6. No reposted screenshots of other accounts. Link and credit the handle.
7. Photos only from the license whitelist: NASA, SpaceX on Flickr, ESA and
   Wikimedia with an allowed license tag, and consented club photos. Nothing
   else, no matter how good it looks.
8. Every caption carries a source line.
9. Uncertain date, place, deadline, eligibility, or cost means the flag
   "confirm before posting", never a guess.
10. No claims about the school, district, teachers, or other students.
11. Never post a student's contact details, home, or schedule. Club meeting
    room and time at school are fine.
12. No paid promotions, no affiliate links, no fundraising asks without an
    officer's explicit request in the plan.
